# Fictional Incident Reporting SOP

This SOP belongs to the imaginary company Cloudberry Example Co. It is training material, not a real policy.

## Report a technology incident

To report a technology incident, submit a ticket to the fictional service desk.
Include the time, affected service, observed symptoms, and business impact.
Do not include passwords or other secrets in the incident ticket.

## Urgent service outage

For an urgent service outage affecting multiple employees, call the fictional on-call coordinator using the internal directory.
Record the ticket number and the time of your call.
This sample SOP does not specify a phone number or a guaranteed response time.
