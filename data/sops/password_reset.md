# Fictional Password Reset SOP

This SOP belongs to the imaginary company Cloudberry Example Co. It is training material, not a real policy.

## Reset a forgotten password

To reset a forgotten password, open the fictional Cloudberry account portal and select Reset password.
Enter your company email address. Follow the reset link sent to that address.
Choose a new password and sign in again.

## Missing reset email

If the reset email has not arrived after ten minutes, check your spam folder.
If it is still missing, contact the fictional service desk at helpdesk@example.invalid.
Never send your password to the service desk.
