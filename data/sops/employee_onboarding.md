# Fictional Employee Onboarding SOP

This SOP belongs to the imaginary company Cloudberry Example Co. It is training material, not a real policy.

## Before the first day

The hiring manager submits the employee onboarding checklist to the fictional People Operations team.
People Operations confirms the employee start date and assigns an onboarding buddy.

## First day equipment

On the first day, the employee collects a laptop from the fictional IT desk.
The onboarding buddy helps the employee sign in and find the orientation schedule.
The employee reports missing equipment to the hiring manager.
